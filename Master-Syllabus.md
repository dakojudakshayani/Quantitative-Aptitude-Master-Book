# QUALITY AND COMPLETENESS RULES

- Teach each mathematics topic in one primary chapter.
- Use cross-references instead of duplicate lessons.
- Teach foundations before advanced applications.
- Label optional and advanced topics clearly.
- Do not imply that every topic is required for every examination.
- Verify examination coverage against the latest official notification.
- Keep logical reasoning separate.
- Keep verbal ability separate.
- Keep communication skills separate.
- Keep programming and technical subjects separate.
- Store detailed explanations, examples and question banks in individual chapter files.
- Maintain consistent chapter numbering and Markdown formatting.
