# Quantitative Aptitude Master Book

## Master Syllabus and Learning Roadmap

**Purpose:** A beginner-friendly, logic-first mathematics and
quantitative aptitude resource for placement examinations and government
competitive examinations.

**Learning principle:** Understand → Practise → Recall → Revise → Test →
Master.

This syllabus is the master checklist. Topics will be taught in
prerequisite order. A topic is marked complete only after explanation,
varied practice, revision and a mastery check.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How to use this syllabus

-   Start with Book 1 and follow the sequence. Do not skip prerequisites
    just because a later topic looks more interesting.
-   Theory will be short and useful: meaning, purpose, rules, conditions
    and common mistakes.
-   Every chapter will include worked examples, more than one solving
    method where useful, question variations, practice sets and a short
    revision sheet.
-   Keep an error log: write the question type, your mistake, the
    correct logic and one similar question.
-   Revisit topics on Day 1, Day 2, Day 4, Day 7, Day 15 and Day 30.
    Adjust the schedule when a topic is still difficult.
-   Speed comes after accuracy and understanding.

## Important scope notes

-   This library focuses on mathematics and quantitative aptitude.
-   Verbal ability and logical reasoning are separate subjects and are
    not mixed into this book.
-   Some exams place topics such as number series or quadratic
    comparison in different sections. They are included here as
    exam-oriented quantitative question types where relevant.
-   Not every examination asks every topic. The master book is broad;
    exam-specific study plans will select the relevant topics from it.
-   We will audit the syllabus against current official exam
    notifications when building a specific exam plan.
