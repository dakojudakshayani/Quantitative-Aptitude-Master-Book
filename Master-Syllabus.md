Mathematics & Quantitative Aptitude Master Book
Master Syllabus -- Version 2.0
Purpose: A structured, beginner-to-advanced mathematics and
quantitative aptitude resource for campus placements and relevant
competitive examinations.
Scope boundary: This repository covers mathematics and quantitative
aptitude only. Logical reasoning, verbal ability, communication skills,
programming and core technical subjects belong in separate learning
resources.
Important exam note: The master syllabus is broader than any one
examination. A topic being included here does not mean every examination
requires it. Always check the latest official notification and syllabus
for the examination and post you are targeting. Where an authority does
not publish a chapter-wise list, exam tags are preparation guidance
rather than official claims.
---
How to use this master syllabus
Each chapter should follow this learning sequence:
Purpose and simple definition
Prerequisites
Concept and step-by-step logic
Rules, formulas and the conditions in which they apply
Worked examples
Different valid solving methods
Easy, intermediate, difficult and tricky variations
Placement and relevant competitive-exam questions
Common mistakes and answer-checking methods
Practice set, chapter test and answer explanations
Short revision notes and spaced review
Mastery rule: Do not mark a topic complete merely because you have
read it. You should be able to explain the concept, solve unfamiliar
variations, check your answer and recall the method during later
revision.

Practice design
For each important concept, include: - Basic understanding questions -
Direct application questions - Word problems - Reverse and missing-value
questions - Multi-step and mixed-concept questions - Tricky questions
that test understanding - Exam-style questions with reliable source
labels
Do not invent previous-year labels. Original questions must be marked as
original practice questions.
Quality and scope rules
Teach each topic in one primary chapter; use cross-references
instead of duplicating lessons.
Keep foundational concepts before applications.
Keep optional advanced mathematics clearly labelled.
Do not imply that every topic is required by every exam.
Verify current exam coverage using the latest official notification
or syllabus.
Record the official source and date checked for exam-specific
mappings.
Keep logical reasoning, verbal ability, English grammar,
communication, programming and technical subjects outside this
repository.
Prefer clear explanations and sufficient practice over unnecessary
theory or inflated chapter counts.
