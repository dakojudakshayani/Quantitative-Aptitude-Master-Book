Master Syllabus – Version 2.1
Purpose: A beginner-to-advanced roadmap for mathematics and quantitative aptitude used in campus placements and relevant competitive examinations.
Scope: Mathematics and quantitative aptitude only. Logical reasoning, verbal ability, communication, programming and core technical subjects are maintained separately.
Important: This is a broad master syllabus, not the official syllabus of every examination. Check the latest official notification to decide which topics apply to your target exam.
---
How to Use This Syllabus
Follow the chapters in order unless your exam pathway requires a different sequence.
Understand concepts before memorizing shortcuts.
Each chapter will later have separate detailed notes, examples, practice questions and tests.
Exam tags are study guidance, not guarantees that a topic appears in every exam.

Quality Rules
Teach each topic in one primary chapter; use cross-references instead of duplicate lessons.
Teach foundations before applications.
Label optional advanced topics clearly.
Do not imply every topic is required by every exam.
Verify exam coverage against the latest official notification before calling it official.
Keep logical reasoning, verbal ability, communication, programming and technical subjects outside this repository.
Keep this master syllabus concise. Store detailed explanations, examples and question banks in separate chapter files.
Publication status: This is the master learning roadmap. Exam-specific mappings require notification-level verification and should record the official source and date checked.
